About Me

I am a research-oriented Pharmacy student at the University of Rajshahi with a passion for pharmaceutical sciences and drug discovery. My work focuses on understanding how bioactive natural compounds can be translated into therapeutic agents.

My research interests span clinical pharmacology, natural product-based drug discovery, and anticancer drug research. I'm particularly fascinated by the potential of plant-derived compounds against cancer and their molecular mechanisms.

I'm developing expertise in research methodology, laboratory techniques, scientific writing, and evidence-based analysis—skills essential for contributing meaningfully to innovative pharmaceutical research.
